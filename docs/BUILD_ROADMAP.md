# Development Roadmap

## ForgeFront Factory Resilience
**ForgeFront Systems | Wake Industries**

ForgeFront Factory Resilience connects manufacturing evidence, material traceability, production schedules, and recovery decisions into one operational intelligence experience.

The roadmap moves from a working factory scenario to an investigation and recovery demonstration for the **Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon 2026**.

### Stage 1 | Foundation and design
**October 17–25, 2026**

Develop a repeatable manufacturing environment with consistent production records and measurable incident behavior. Establish the evidence model, benchmark scenarios, operator workflow, and competition entry requirements.

**Delivery:** A working 40-order factory scenario, traceable material discrepancies, four recovery approaches, and a clear specification for the next stage.

### Stage 2 | Document intelligence
**October 26–28, 2026**

Bring certificates, purchase orders, material records, routing data, and customer commitments into a coherent evidence model. Integrate Meta AI to interpret cross-document relationships and communicate supported findings.

**Delivery:** Cross-document investigation with source attribution, ambiguity handling, and measured AI-generated explanations.

### Stage 3 | Consequence and recovery intelligence
**October 28–30, 2026**

Expand the dependency graph from materials to production jobs and delivery commitments. Compare recovery strategies using available inventory, lead times, priorities, and machine capacity.

**Delivery:** Clear impact mapping and recovery plans that respond to changing factory conditions.

### Stage 4 | Operational command experience
**October 30–November 1, 2026**

Connect investigation, production consequence, scenario planning, and operator decisions into one continuous workflow. Preserve incident decisions and make complex operational choices immediately understandable.

**Delivery:** A cohesive, responsive command interface with incident history and an evidence report.

### Stage 5 | Validation and demonstration
**November 1–3, 2026**

Exercise the system against unexpected conditions, conduct desktop and mobile trials, refine the live experience, and prepare the competition narrative.

**Delivery:** An end-to-end factory recovery experience, evaluated scenarios, and a judge presentation.

### Stage 6 | Submission and next chapter
**November 4–9, 2026**

Assemble the competition release, submit through the official event process, preserve the reference build, and capture feedback for the longer-term product direction.

**Delivery:** Competition materials and a defined foundation for future manufacturing pilots.

## The demonstration story

A manufacturing team discovers a conflicting material certificate. The system identifies the connected production work, displays the evidence, calculates recovery alternatives, and responds when an additional factory constraint changes.
